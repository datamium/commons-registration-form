Thank you. Your registration and documents have been received successfully. Here is exactly what happens next.

  1. We verify your submission. Our team confirms your payment and checks your identification document. This usually takes a couple of business days.
  2. We set up your access. Your photo is registered in the Face ID system so the space recognises you at any hour.
  3. You receive your access details. We email you confirmation of your place, the dates of the four weekends, the address at Commons Zerktouni (Mers Sultan), and how to get in.
