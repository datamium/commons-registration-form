PAWN × Commons · 4 Weekends Sprint

Apply to move.

In Casablanca, the bottleneck isn't ambition; it's a place to actually do the work. Cafés close, home distracts, offices shut at 6. This summer, for four weekends, that problem is solved.

  • Open 24 hours, not 9-to-6. Work the late nights and early mornings, whenever you focus best. The space never closes on you.
  • 8 full days, 4 weekends. A real block of time to ship something, at Commons Zerktouni in central Casablanca (Mers Sultan).
  • One flat price: 360 MAD. No membership, no contract, no per-day math.

A day pass in Casablanca runs 100 to 250 MAD, and only for business hours. This is 8 days, around the clock, for 360 MAD. Roughly 45 dirhams a day.

Registration Received

Thank you. Your registration and documents have been received successfully. Here is exactly what happens next.

  1. We verify your submission. Our team confirms your payment and checks your identification document. This usually takes a couple of business days.
  2. We set up your access. Your photo is registered in the Face ID system so the space recognises you at any hour.
  3. You receive your access details. We email you confirmation of your place, the dates of the four weekends, the address at Commons Zerktouni (Mers Sultan), and how to get in.
